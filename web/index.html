Why CHOMS

Built around the child, not the paperwork

Outreach teams lose time to notebooks, spreadsheets and phone calls. CHOMS puts the whole story of a child in one place that the right people can reach.

Know every child

A case file for each child holds identity, guardian consent, every visit, every referral and every appointment, searchable in seconds.

No referral is forgotten

Urgent and emergency referrals stay at the top of the dashboard until the clinic outcome is recorded. Missed ones are visible to the administrator.

Reports you can stand behind

Coordinators prepare funder reports for any date range, administrators approve them, and every change leaves an audit trail.

Roles

The right access for the right person

The more sensitive the action, the fewer people who can do it. The database enforces this, not just the screens.

Pending

Just registered

Anyone can create an account. It stays locked until an administrator approves it.

  • Create an account on the website, web app or phone
  • Sign-up is logged in the audit trail
  • Cannot sign in or see any records
Community health worker

In the field

Sees only the children assigned to them.

  • Register and edit own clients
  • Record visits and referrals
  • Book appointments
  • No reports, users or audit log
Coordinator

Running the programme

Sees all fieldwork across the team.

  • View every client, visit and referral
  • Generate and save funder reports
  • Cannot change records or manage users
Administrator

In charge

Full control with a full audit trail.

  • Approve new accounts and set roles; edit and delete users
  • Assign clients, visits and appointments to CHWs
  • Approve reports, read the audit log
  • Delete records
  • Read the audit log
Safeguarding and privacy

Children's information deserves the strongest protection

Protection sits on the server, where a modified app cannot skip it.

Approval before access

Anyone can request an account, but nobody sees a single record until an administrator approves them.

Rules enforced by the database

A health worker's request for another worker's children is refused by Firestore itself.

Consent first

No consent, no record. The rules reject a registration that has no consent, even from a modified app.

Everything is accountable

Every sign-up, approval, create, update and delete is logged with who and when. The database refuses a change that has no log entry, and log entries cannot be edited.

firestore.rules
// a new account starts locked as 'pending', and the sign-up must be logged
allow create: if request.auth.uid == userId
  && request.resource.data.role == 'pending'
  && audited('users', userId, ['register']);

// a user only reaches their own visits
allow read: if isAdmin()
  || ownsExisting('chwId');

Excerpt of the rules published with the project.

Questions

Straight answers

Can anyone sign up and see children's records?

No. Anyone can request an account, but a new account has no access until an administrator approves it. Until then the database refuses every read and write.

Does it work without internet?

The web app keeps a local copy and a queue of changes, so a worker can record visits with no signal and the changes sync when the connection returns. The Android app needs a connection.

Are SMS and email reminders sent automatically?

Not yet. Each appointment stores the reminder channel and the time it is due, 24 hours before. Sending messages needs a server function on a paid Firebase plan, which is listed as future work.

Can one health worker see another worker's children?

No. A health worker's queries are limited to their own ID, and the database rules reject anything else. Coordinators and administrators see everyone.

Who creates the first administrator?

The first administrator is created once in the Firebase console during setup. After that, administrators approve everyone else from the Users page.

Where is the data stored?

In Cloud Firestore inside your own Firebase project. The region is chosen when the database is created and cannot be changed later, so choose Johannesburg when it is offered.
